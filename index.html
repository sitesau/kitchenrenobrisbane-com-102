Kitchen Renovation Brisbane Costs Explained

The first decision is scope rather than finishes. A makeover, a standard renovation and a custom project sit in different cost and timing bands. For a kitchen renovation brisbane project, understanding these distinctions helps set realistic expectations. A makeover typically involves refreshing surfaces and fixtures without moving structural elements, often sitting at the lower end of the budget range. A standard renovation might include moving appliances or changing the layout, while a custom project involves bespoke cabinetry and significant structural changes. The source material notes that the published range of about $15,000 to $60,000+ is best treated as a scoping signal, not a promise. When one quote is much higher or lower than another, ask whether the difference sits in demolition, service changes, cabinetry detail, benchtop selection, lighting or appliance integration.

Where the Money Goes

Cabinetry is usually the single largest expense, at roughly 25 to 40 per cent of the total budget. This makes cabinet layout, internal storage and finish specification more important to compare than small accessory items. Overhead storage, pull out systems, corner solutions and walk in pantry designs are all named on the source page, so they are fair items to ask about when the room needs harder working storage. The source page mentions silica free stone alternatives, durable laminate options and premium porcelain surfaces. It also says traditional engineered quartz is now banned due to silicosis concerns. For a practical quote review, ask what the selected surface changes in the price, the look of the room and the rest of the installation. The source site context notes that partners include Blum certified installers, ensuring your cabinetry features the world's leading soft-close mechanisms.

Timeline Expectations

The source page gives three planning bands, 2 to 4 weeks for makeovers, 4 to 8 weeks for standard renovations and 8 to 12 weeks for custom projects. Those bands help turn a quote into a real life plan. If the household needs cooking access, coordinated appliance delivery or a clear completion window, ask which assumptions sit behind the proposed timeframe. A compact apartment kitchen and an older detached house can raise different questions about layout, storage and trade sequencing, which may impact the timeline.

Compliance and Licensing

Licensing is critical. Queensland compliance checks are not something to leave until the end of the process. The source page states that every listed specialist holds a current QBCC licence, and it says a QBCC licence is required for building work over $3,300 in Queensland. It also says licensed plumbers and electricians are required by Queensland law. For an owner, those details work as quote filters. Ask which licence details apply to the job, which parts of the work are being handled by plumbing and electrical trades, and whether the quote is fixed price or contains allowances that could move later. The same source page says workmanship warranty is included, so it is reasonable to ask how warranty issues, defects and variations are recorded before work starts.

Return on Investment

If resale is part of the decision, the source page says kitchens are consistently ranked highly by buyers and that metro Brisbane kitchen renovations typically deliver 60 to 80 per cent return on investment. That is a planning signal only. It helps explain why owners often focus on durability, storage and layout rather than purely decorative upgrades. Real estate agents consistently rank the kitchen as the most important room for resale value, with 80% of buyers placing it in their top three priorities.
